# The 7 conversations & their question arcs

One page for approval. Each chat carries a fixed **arc** of intents — the things
that must get resolved for that part of the idea to be real. Intents are
identical for every idea (uniform across the platform); the *wording* and the
*answer options* are generated per idea, so it always sounds custom.

`■ required` — the chat isn't "covered" until these are answered.
`□ optional` — depth, asked only as the conversation continues.
`(S)` singular — one answer, a new one replaces it. `(A)` accumulative — answers stack.

---

## 1 · The problem
*What's broken today, who feels it, and what it costs them*

| | Intent | |
|---|---|---|
| ■ | Who feels this most sharply? | (S) |
| ■ | How do they cope today — the workaround? | (A) |
| ■ | What does it cost them — time, money, or risk? | (S) |
| □ | How often does it bite? | (S) |
| □ | Why hasn't someone fixed it already? | (S) |

*(Onboarding pre-answers "what's broken" — never re-asked.)*

## 7 · Think out loud
*Anything else on your mind — it all gets filed automatically*

No arc. Free-form; everything said here is captured and filed into the buckets
above. Open questions from the other chats surface here.
